How to get started?

Different situations justify different solutions. I work together with you to find an agreement which fits the most important expectation: yours.

My typical engagement workflow:

  1. I learn about your business and current challenges
  2. Assess the current situation, goals, and constraints
  3. Find a matching solution for your situation
  4. Support you until feeling confident to continue without me

At each step you decide how to continue with the identified opportunities: address them internally, keep working together, or use other providers.
