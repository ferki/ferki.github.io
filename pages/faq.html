How to get started?

Since each goal and situation is different, I prefer to work together on finding an agreement that fits the most important expectation: yours. The typical workflow is the following:

  1. Book an appointment to chat about how I may contribute to your success.

  2. Discover all the crucial details together. If no project brief is available yet, I’m glad to create one with you during a workshop.

  3. Get a personalized offer based on the project brief.

What does the project brief include?

It makes sure we have a shared understanding of the important details, allowing to make a personalized offer matching your needs. Ideally your first inquiry already includes these details, while I’m also happy to explore and document your situation together in a workshop.

It contains the following sections:

  • company profile, context
  • project goals and scope
  • team and key roles
  • intended process and methodology
  • expected deliverables
  • technical requirements
  • project budget
  • timeline, deadlines
  • dependencies
  • contractual details

Where?

The overwhelming majority of my work is de facto remote. I also travel often from my base in Berlin, Germany, which allows me to flexibly choose a location that fits any given situation the best.

When?

My standard working hours are between 10:00 and 18:00 on workdays according to my local timezone and calendar.

How much?

I prepare personalized offers to match your exact situation. In case you need a number for initial planning purposes, my typical remote service fee is €890 per day.

On-site service and work outside standard working hours are subject to availability, surcharge, and/or travel expense reimbursement.

My standard fees are reassessed periodically to ensure fair offers and a sustainable service quality on a constantly changing market.

The above rates are correct at the time of publication, and exclude any VAT which may apply. Discounts for long-term, pay-in-advance, and free/libre/open source orders may be discussed. Errors and omissions excepted.
