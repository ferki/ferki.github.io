PostgreSQL assessment

I solve reliability, performance, and cost challenges related to PostgreSQL, typically dealing with terabyte-scale deployments.

My evidence-based comprehensive approach identifies bottlenecks to create an actionable improvement plan. On top of that, I work together with your team during the process to roll out changes, and tune configuration settings.

It enables continuously acting on new information, benefiting early and gradually from improvements, while also sharing how to keep the database tidy.

This solution includes the following key areas:

  1. Check data safety and business continuity settings and processes
  2. Identify usage patterns, slow queries, and their bottlenecks
  3. Check cache hit ratio for indexes and tables
  4. Fit high-level CPU, RAM, storage, and network settings to the given use case
  5. Remove unused indexes, add missing indexes, and optimize remaining indexes
  6. Check logs for both expected and unusual patterns
  7. Review locks and timeout settings
  8. Identify table and index bloat
  9. Tidy up with VACUUM/ANALYZE/pg_squeeze as needed
  10. Ensure autovacuum settings can keep the database tidy

Such an assessment typically takes 2-4 weeks, and pricing starts at €8900.
